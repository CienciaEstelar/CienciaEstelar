## 🧭 About Me

I work at the intersection of **physics, computation and engineering**. My focus is not just on building models, but on designing **complete, reproducible systems**: from data ingestion and numerical solvers to validation, visualization and interpretation.

My projects span:

* Computational physics and cosmology
* Gaussian Processes and scientific machine learning
* Industrial-grade data pipelines and soft-sensors
* High-quality scientific visualization
* STEM education and public outreach

I value **physical consistency, interpretability and robustness** over black-box performance.
